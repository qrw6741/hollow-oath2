# World Bible — seed draft v0.1 (for creative director review)

Everything here is a proposal to be approved, changed or cut. Only the items marked **[in game]** are currently referenced by content.

## Premise
The kingdom of **Vaelmoor** was held together by the **Hollow Oath** — a vow every subject swore to the Crown at coming of age, binding a sliver of their soul to the throne. On the **Night of Unbinding**, the oath inverted: the sworn did not die, but whatever the oath held was emptied out. The royal records say a plague; the truth was buried with the court.

The player is an **Oathbound** **[in game]** — a knight whose oath-mark is cracked rather than hollow, the only sworn soul still able to choose. They wake at the first **Oath Candle** **[in game]**.

## Core mysteries (unresolved, revealed across regions)
1. Who broke the oath — and was it broken, or *completed*?
2. Why the player's oath cracked instead of hollowing.
3. What sits on the Hollow Throne now.

## Key concepts
- **Oath Candles [in game]** — votive shrines where villagers renewed their vow; resting re-kindles a thread of the oath (checkpoint, revives the hollowed).
- **Ember Vials [in game]** — candle-wax reliquaries holding a borrowed warmth (healing).
- **Hollow Penitents [in game]** — commoners who swore on the Night of Unbinding; they still perform the gestures of penance with whatever blade they hold.

## Factions (draft)
- **The Crown of Vaelmoor** — absent; its chancellors sealed the Citadel.
- **The Candlewrights** — the order that kept the candles and wrote the oath liturgy; suspected authors of the Unbinding.
- **The Unsworn** — those who refused the oath and were exiled to the Bleeding Gardens; the only sane survivors.
- **The Reliquary Engineers** — built mechanisms to store oath-fragments in the Iron Reliquary.

## Regions (target map)
1. Forsaken Village · 2. Drowned Catacombs · 3. Ashen Cathedral · 4. Bleeding Gardens · 5. Iron Reliquary · 6. Sunken Basilica · 7. Crownless Citadel · 8. The Hollow Throne

The test arena, **The Penitent's Yard [in game]**, is planned to become the churchyard at the edge of the Forsaken Village.

## Timeline (draft)
- ~300 years before: first oath sworn at the founding of Vaelmoor.
- 40 years before: Candlewrights rewrite the liturgy.
- The Night of Unbinding.
- An unknown span later: the Oathbound wakes.
