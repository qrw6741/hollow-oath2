# World Bible — seed draft v0.1 (for creative director review)

Everything here is a proposal to be approved, changed or cut. Only the items marked **[in game]** are currently referenced by content.

## Premise
The kingdom of **Vaelmoor** was held together by the **Hollow Oath** — a vow every subject swore to the Crown at coming of age, binding a sliver of their soul to the throne. On the **Night of Unbinding**, the oath inverted: the sworn did not die, but whatever the oath held was emptied out. The royal records say a plague; the truth was buried with the court.

The player is an **Oathbound** **[in game]** — a knight whose oath-mark is cracked rather than hollow, the only sworn soul still able to choose. They wake at the first **Oath Candle** **[in game]**.

## The prologue (draft, in game)
- The first three areas are **the Hollow [in game]**: the dream the oath built around the sworn on the Night of Unbinding — the oath remembering itself. Defeating the Drowned Canon breaks it: a light opens and the Oathbound wakes in **Vaelmoor, Awake [in game]**, the real kingdom under a real sun. The Waking Stone says so; the road onward leads toward the Crownless Citadel.

## After the prologue (draft, in game)
- **The Vaelmoor Road [in game]** — the first real-world area, east of Vaelmoor, Awake: Pilgrim's Rest, the Crow Fields, the Ford and the Marshal's Camp, ending at the crossroads where Sir Orren holds the road. The road onward to the Crownless Citadel is sealed in this build.
- **Ommen [in game]** — the hooded figure who tends the candles on the road. A Candlewright who signed the rewritten liturgy forty years before the Night and did not read it closely enough. He tells the story a page at a time, each time you speak to him (first as "Hooded Figure", then by name). He knows whose hand wrote the Candlewright's note and will not say.
- **Wenna the Pedlar [in game]** — a living trader who walks the road because it is the only thing in Vaelmoor that still pays. Sells bandages, tonics and Pilgrim's Draughts for embers.

## Core mysteries (unresolved, revealed across regions)
1. Who broke the oath — and was it broken, or *completed*?
2. Why the player's oath cracked instead of hollowing.
3. What sits on the Hollow Throne now.

## Key concepts
- **Oath Candles [in game]** — votive shrines where villagers renewed their vow; resting re-kindles a thread of the oath (checkpoint, revives the hollowed).
- **Ember Vials [in game]** — candle-wax reliquaries holding a borrowed warmth (healing).
- **Hollow Penitents [in game]** — commoners who swore on the Night of Unbinding; they still perform the gestures of penance with whatever blade they hold.

## Factions (draft)
- **The Crown of Vaelmoor** — absent; its chancellors sealed the Citadel.
- **The Candlewrights** — the order that kept the candles and wrote the oath liturgy; suspected authors of the Unbinding.
- **The Unsworn** — those who refused the oath and were exiled to the Bleeding Gardens; the only sane survivors.
- **The Reliquary Engineers** — built mechanisms to store oath-fragments in the Iron Reliquary.

## Regions (target map)
1. Forsaken Village · 2. Drowned Catacombs · 3. Ashen Cathedral · 4. Bleeding Gardens · 5. Iron Reliquary · 6. Sunken Basilica · 7. Crownless Citadel · 8. The Hollow Throne

The test arena, **The Penitent's Yard [in game]**, is the churchyard at the edge of the **Forsaken Village [in game]**, reached through the Bell Door.

## Timeline (draft)
- ~300 years before: first oath sworn at the founding of Vaelmoor.
- 40 years before: Candlewrights rewrite the liturgy.
- The Night of Unbinding.
- An unknown span later: the Oathbound wakes.

## Enemies
- **Ember Acolyte [in game]** — Candlewright novices still tending censers of a liturgy no one hears; the burning wax they fling was meant to seal oaths.
- **Bell-Bearer [in game]** — penitents who hauled the village bells to the chapel on the Night of Unbinding and never set them down.
- **Gloam Moth [in game]** — moths that drank from the candles after the Unbinding; they circle anything that burns and dive at it.
- **Oathguard [in game]** — sworn shields of the Vaelmoor road. Their oath was to hold the line; no one told them the line was gone.
- **Mimic [in game]** — a strongbox that learned to wait. It breathes; real chests do not.

## Bosses
- **The Bell-Warden [in game]** — the churchyard's bell-ringer, who swore first and loudest on the Night of Unbinding. He still keeps the yard and wears the cracked Yard Bell as a helm so that nothing else will ever ring it; he fights with its iron clapper. Intro boss at the end of the Penitent's Yard. Drops the **Warden's Bell Shard [in game]**.

- **The Drowned Canon [in game]** — a Candlewright canon who read the liturgy backwards in the catacomb chapel on the Night of Unbinding; the crypt flooded while he was still reading, and he has not stopped. Boss of the **Drowned Catacombs [in game]**, under the village chapel. Drops the **Canon's Wax Seal [in game]** (the Candlewright mark pressed upside down), which answers to the stair to the Ashen Cathedral.

- **Sir Orren, the Hollow Marshal [in game]** — Marshal of the Crown's road. He swore to hold the crossroads against anything that came, and has held it against everything since. Lance, couched charge, and a tattered banner that calls the road's crows. Boss at the end of the Vaelmoor Road. Drops the **Marshal's Crest [in game]**.
- **Carrion Crow [in game]**, **Roadside Pikeman [in game]** — the road's daylight enemies: crows that follow the road for what falls on it, and conscripts of the road watch whose courage "was told to follow".

## Items written so far
- **Candlewright's Note [in game]** — 'On the night, the liturgy is to be read from its last line to its first. The sworn will not notice. The Crown must not.' (first hint at mystery 1)
- **Oathblade [in game]** — "A plain straight sword issued to every sworn knight of the old kingdom. The engraving on its fuller has been scratched away by its last owner."

## Hobb, the Blacksmith and Sister Ilse, the Vial-Keeper
Two more of the road's survivors, each at both the edge of Vaelmoor and Pilgrim's Rest. **Hobb** was a farrier, then the garrison's smith; he works for whoever is still walking, asks only for scrap iron and coal-money, and will make something new from the relic of anything great you have killed - he only needs to *see* it. **Sister Ilse** keeps the Ember Vials, taught by the Candlewrights to bottle the last warmth of a fire; she strengthens them with Hollow Resin, the amber sap that weeps from things that should be dead.

## Hollowmere Manor
The old house of the Dowager, later the Candlewrights', later nobody's. When the oath turned over, the halls emptied and then filled again with those who could not leave: ghosts of the household, and the road's few survivors who found the way in. **Aldous Vane**, the steward, is dead and keeping house regardless. Ommen's Hollowmere Sigil - a Candlewright's wax seal, a candle and a broken crown - opens the road to it. The Gallery at the far end is sealed; the house has not decided who is to be let in.
